Abstract

A Practice for the Threshold

Plasma fields, Schumann shifts, collapsing institutions — something is ending and something is arriving. The cosmology is real. Most of us still don't know what to do with our nervous systems on Tuesday morning.

The Age of Ego runs on a script. The Age of Love runs on a practice — five steps that meet you where you actually live: in the kitchen, in the marriage, in the triggered moment that came out of nowhere.

This is not a talk about the shift. It is a fifteen-minute walk through the practice that brings the shift twelve inches down — from cosmology in the head to alignment in the body.

Bring something hard from your life. Leave with the framework that meets it.

Bio

Kindred Lights

Kindred Lights is the author of What Happened to US? From the Age of Ego to the Age of Love and Love Heroically: The Five Steps to Freedom, the first two books of The Hero's Path of Love series, and the creator of the Five Steps practice and the Luminology framework underneath them.

The work was not built in a retreat center or a graduate program. It was built in custody courtrooms, on jail floors, and in ten thousand ordinary moments where the lower self wanted to burn the world down — and the higher self said open your heart, I'll show you what to do.

Behind the imprint: a Carnegie Mellon MBA. A multi-decade nonprofit leadership career. Twenty years of recovery work. Certification in Resilient Heart Trauma-Sensitive HeartMath. Yoga teacher training. A Thanksgiving table rebuilt from the wreckage of three DUIs, a narcissistic marriage, and a custody battle that cost daily access to the children for years — with a seat at the head of that table for the man who had once been the adversary, and three children now thriving.

A record of building things that have to function in the world while doing the inner work that has to function in the soul.

Colin Tipping, creator of Radical Forgiveness, called the work "one of the most powerful demonstrations of my work I have ever witnessed."

Not a guru. Not a master. A mapmaker — for an Age that needs maps.
