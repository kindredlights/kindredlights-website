A Circle of Kindred Lights

We are walking this together, hand in hand.

The circle is gathering.

Kindred lights are being invited to share their first entries — moments of walking the Five Steps, catching Sir Ego at his antics, being held in ways that became visible only in retrospect.

This is not a forum. It is not a social network. It is a witness wall — a place where kindred lights share what they have noticed, so that those who come after can see they are not walking this alone.

What You Will Find Here

Three kinds of accounts, when the circle opens:

The Practice
Five Steps in Action

A moment when a kindred light walked the practice — the trigger, the steps run, what shifted. The data points that show the practice actually working in real lives.

Ego Humor
Catching Sir Ego at His Antics

A moment when a kindred light noticed their own Sir Ego doing something that turned out to be funny in retrospect. Laughing with him, not at him. That's just the way Sir Egos are — we all have them.

Held
Mission Control Moments

A moment when a kindred light realized, looking back, that they had been held — that something larger had been arranging the timing all along. The held-ness that becomes visible only in retrospect.
